# Milestone 1: Community Observations

## Community

**Source:** r/Games  
**Discussion format:** Weekly gaming discussion — “What have you been playing, and what are your thoughts?”  
**Research status:** Preliminary observations; candidate distinctions are not final labels.

## Purpose

Explore public gaming discussions to identify recurring patterns in how people share experiences, evaluate games, explain opinions, and interact with other players.

## Observed Examples

### Example 1: Control 2

The author evaluates combat, enemy variety, exploration, navigation, and storytelling, comparing the game with Control 1.

**Observations:** Detailed reasoning, comparisons, positive and negative reactions, and explanations of game design.

**Boundary case:** Personal reactions and reasoned analysis occur together.

### Example 2: Monster Hunter Portable 3rd

The author describes completion progress, achievement requirements, estimated playtime, and frustration with a boss fight.

**Observations:** Progress reporting, numerical estimates, personal opinions, and supporting explanations.

**Boundary case:** A personal progress report can contain substantial reasoning.

### Example 3: The Outer Worlds 2 and Moonlighter

The author discusses combat, achievements, weapons, game progression, and recommendations.

**Observations:** Personal experience, evaluation, emotional reactions, detailed gameplay examples, and recommendations.

**Boundary case:** One post can serve several communication purposes.

### Example 4: The Witcher 3

The author praises quest writing and preparation-based combat while criticizing combat rhythm and enemy encounters.

**Observations:** Balanced evaluation, comparisons, explanations, personal reactions, and progress reporting.

**Boundary case:** A strong overall opinion can include both positive and negative evidence.

### Example 5: Breath of Fire: Dragon Quarter

The author explains how understanding the D-Counter system changes their interpretation of the game's progression and survival mechanics.

**Observations:** Game-mechanic explanations, comparisons with traditional JRPGs, and interpretation of design decisions.

**Boundary case:** Explanatory discussion may overlap with general game analysis.

### Example 6: Monsters & Memories

The author evaluates an early-access MMORPG, describing its immersion, profession system, social gameplay, grinding, and subscription cost.

**Observations:** Feature-based analysis, personal experience, balanced evaluation, and recommendations.

**Boundary case:** Recommendations may be part of a broader review rather than a separate purpose.

### Example 7: Breath of Fire: Dragon Quarter discussion thread

The original post explains game mechanics, while the replies share a different experience, ask a question, and clarify how the game is being played.

**Observations:** Explanation, personal experience, question-asking, and technical clarification.

**Boundary case:** Different messages in the same thread can have different purposes.

## Preliminary Candidate Distinctions

These distinctions are hypotheses to test, not final labels.

1. **Reasoned analysis vs. unsupported opinion:** Does the author explain or support a claim with examples, comparisons, or evidence?
2. **Game evaluation vs. personal progress report:** Is the main purpose to evaluate a game or describe personal progress and achievements?
3. **Recommendation vs. general discussion:** Is the author primarily advising others what to play or do, or sharing thoughts and experiences?
4. **Question-seeking vs. information-sharing:** Is the author primarily requesting input or providing information and interpretation?

## Questions for Further Investigation

- Can these distinctions be applied consistently to individual posts?
- Which distinctions overlap too much to work as separate categories?
- Should a post be classified by its primary purpose rather than every feature it contains?
- Are short opinions and direct requests for advice distinguishable from detailed reviews?
- Should each dataset example be an individual post or comment rather than a complete discussion thread?

## Next Steps

- Refine the candidate distinctions using the broader set of posts reviewed.
- Select a small number of clear, distinct labels and write decision rules.
- Define five measurable acceptance criteria before training.
- Commit research notes and subsequent milestones separately.
